## 🟢 OPTION 1: PROMPT FOR AI AVATAR GENERATORS (HeyGen, Synthesia)
Use this if you are using an AI avatar to speak your script.

**Settings / Instructions:**
- **Avatar Style:** Professional, academic, yet approachable (modern tech-casual attire).
- **Voice:** Confident, clear, engaging, medium pacing.
- **Background:** A sleek, modern data science lab, dark mode UI with glowing data points, or a minimalist university gradient.

**Prompt to paste into the Avatar scene generator:**
```text
Create a presentation scene featuring a professional AI avatar delivering a machine learning technical report. The avatar should be standing slightly to the left to leave room for technical slides on the right. 
Background: A modern, sleek dark-themed tech environment with subtle glowing nodes representing a neural network or a connection graph (symbolizing dating app matching).
Mood: Academic, confident, and innovative.
```
*(Note: You will then upload the generated audio/script for the avatar to speak.)*

## 🛠️ HOW TO ASSEMBLE THE VIDEO
1. **Audio/Voice:** Either record your own voices or use ElevenLabs/HeyGen to generate voiceovers using the script.
2. **Visuals:** Generate the background loops using the B-roll prompts above.
3. **Slides:** Create actual slides with your diagrams (Pipeline, Confusion Matrices, SHAP plots).
4. **Editor:** Put them together in CapCut or Premiere Pro. Show the speaker for the intro of their section, then cut to the slides/B-roll while they keep talking.
