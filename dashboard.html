Insight Log
1. Mutual Matches (Relative Weight: 0.38)
By far the strongest binary class predictor. Active mutual matches prove immediate reciprocal user attraction patterns which tree classifiers prioritize to establish positive connection outcomes.
2. Likes Received (Relative Weight: 0.22)
Serves as a robust proxy for profile attractiveness. A high likes-to-swipe ratio heavily drives the classifier towards predicting successful real-world dating outcomes.
3. Relationship Intent (Relative Weight: 0.15)
A strong nominal feature. Profiles highlighting "Serious Relationship" or "Casual Dating" intents demonstrate vastly different connection conversions compared to "Hookups" or "Friends Only" intents.
